# Dataset description.
The initial dataset contained 9,994 rows and 21 columns. The quality assessment identified no missing values, duplicate records, duplicate Row IDs, or invalid date sequences.

The Order Date and Ship Date columns were converted to datetime format, and Postal Code was converted to a string identifier. Negative profit records were retained because they can represent genuine business losses.

The cleaned dataset was saved separately for subsequent KPI calculations, trend analysis, and customer insights.

# Major Findings

## KPI Findings

The sales dataset contains total revenue of $2,297,200.86 across 5,009 distinct orders, with an average order value of $458.61. A total of 37,873 units were sold.

Office Supplies is the top-selling category by quantity sold, while Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier generates the highest total sales revenue among individual products.

These KPIs provide a baseline for understanding business performance. Further analysis is required to examine sales trends over time, regional performance, category contributions, and customer purchasing behavior.
